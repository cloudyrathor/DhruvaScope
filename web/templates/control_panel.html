How to use Visual Tracker Enable Tracker mode, center a known object through the eyepiece or optional camera, select it, and confirm its current position. Add 2–3 points in calibration mode when estimating movement scale. Prepare calculates the next target offset only; it does not move the telescope automatically.

Camera and encoders are optional. Releasing an arrow stops that axis; Emergency stop halts both axes.
